## Goal

Prove, and pin with tests, the two behaviours the audit named: a microphone on `sim` passes the readiness check without a device and the tool shows why; a microphone on `real` without a device still fails after the grace, as it should (a broken device must not look healthy).

## Read first

`deployment/infrastructure/outbound/health/http_health.py` (`UNAVAILABLE_GRACE_SECONDS`, how `reason` is read), `deployment/application/services/deployment_service.py` (the `warning: <reason>` line added for tts, and the rollback of `update`), the existing tests of the readiness wait, T-02.3 of this plan (what the service answers on `sim`).

## Specification

- The service answers on `sim`: `is_available: true`, `reason` = a note that contains `sim` (Q02-2, recommended). The tool prints it as a warning and `deploy` succeeds; `update` does not roll back.
- On `real` with no device the service answers `is_available: false` and the tool fails as before: no change, a test that says so.

## Acceptance criteria

Both tests pass against the fake health endpoint; the deployment suite passes; an end-to-end run with the real service on `sim` is part of T-07.2, not here.

## Verification

Targeted tests, then the whole suite in the background.
